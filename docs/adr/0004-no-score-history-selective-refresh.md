# No score history; selective refresh with an "as of" date

Scores and statistics are overwritten on refresh, with no snapshots or rating history. Refreshes are tiered: the current season weekly, and monthly for this year's Anime plus the top ~1,000 by members. Older Anime are fetched once and documented as stale, and rankings show an "as of" date. A full refresh is a manual run, justified by measuring how much the top-50 rankings change. This keeps the Jikan budget (60 requests a minute) and storage small, at the cost of rankings that compare fresh against stale numbers for less popular titles.
